LGMM INTERACTIVE RESEARCH PRESENTATION

For GitHub Pages, extract the ZIP into the publishing folder with index.html
at its root. No build step or external JavaScript package is required. For local
use, run python3 -m http.server 8000 from this folder and open
http://localhost:8000/ in a modern browser.

The main presentation has 13 scenes. Use the header, the lower-right arrows,
the Left and Right keys, or a horizontal swipe on the slide. Use Sections
or the slide counter to open the roadmap. Present opens the focused layout
and requests fullscreen when supported. In Present, scenes 1–8 reveal one
group at a time with Next, Right, Space, or Page Down. Back, Left, or Page Up
steps backward, and Show all reveals the rest of the current scene. Scenes
9–13 open in full.
Use A− / A+ for projector text size.

- Scene 7 compares the backward characteristic departure point with the
  previous mesh node, first without regularization and then for general
  mesh movement. The two drawings show one backward time step.
- The four Main results cards in Scene 9 open the corresponding statements.
  Next advances directly to the numerical experiment.
- Open Multi-D Error Analysis below Theorem 1 in Error estimates, or from
  the navigation, Sections menu, or Beyond aggregation scene. It contains
  the original v13 sections 13–16.
- Use Sections → Supplement language to switch the X/Y visual, Future
  Directions, and technical dialog controls between English and Japanese.
- Click dotted mathematical terms for definitions and related sections.
  Press Escape to close a focused panel.

- Mesh validity includes contextual mesh-law and Lipschitz seminorm definitions,
  compact illustrations of the two conclusions, a connected-mesh counterexample
  with a signed determinant inspector, and an optional proof insight.
- The final scene distinguishes theoretical research from prospective applications.
